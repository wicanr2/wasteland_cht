## CHARACTER PROFILE（角色名片）

To view any character’s profile -- personal statistics, possessions, and skills -- enter the character’s roster number. When you’re done viewing each screen, press the spacebar <RETURN> to go to the next screen, or <ESC> to return to the previous screen.

要看一個角色的名片——個人數值、身上的東西、技能——輸入他在名單上的編號。看完一頁按空白鍵或 <RETURN> 到下一頁，按 <ESC> 回上一頁。

### ITEMS.（物品）

> 原文小節標題就寫成 `ITEMS.`（全大寫且帶句點），與目錄的 `Items` 不一致，照原樣保留。

The second screen lists what items your character owns. You can carry up to 30 items that you find or buy during your travels. Don’t immediately toss away items that seem useless. It can actually be an important fragment from a greater, more powerful item. Many items can be sold for cash at various stores.

第二頁列出這個角色身上有什麼。旅途中撿到或買到的東西，最多帶三十樣。看起來沒用的別急著丟——它可能是某件更大、更厲害的東西的一塊關鍵零件。很多東西拿到各處商店都賣得掉。

A precaution about picking up items: once you pick something up, you have to keep it or drop it. If you drop it, it will vanish into the depths of Wasteland, never to be seen again. If you have no intention of picking up an item to keep (maybe because you’re already carrying the maximum amount and you don’t want to drop anything) you should leave it where it is. That way, you can come back for it later (and you better remember too, or you may miss out on some loot hidden beneath the item.)

撿東西前先提醒一句：撿起來之後，要嘛留著、要嘛丟掉。丟掉的東西會消失在荒野深處，再也找不回來。如果你本來就沒打算留（也許是身上已經滿了又不想丟掉任何一樣），那就讓它留在原地——之後還回得來拿（而且你最好記得，否則可能錯過壓在那件東西底下的戰利品）。

To give or show something to someone who is not a party member, use the item on the space that you’re on by pressing the spacebar.

要把東西交給或拿給隊伍以外的人看，就站在那一格上按空白鍵，對著那一格使用它。

Press the number that represents the item you’d like to use, and you’ll get the following choices:

|---|---|---|

### Skills（技能）

The third screen shows this character’s skills and skill levels. The skills you posses weigh heavily in your success. Each character should have a slightly different repertoire, so the whole party benefits from a greater variety of skills. One character could specialize in gambling, lockpicking and forgery, while another could excel in demolition, brawling, and climbing.

第三頁顯示這個角色的技能與等級。你會不會成功，很大一部分看你有哪些技能。每個角色的專長最好略有不同，全隊才能受惠於較齊全的技能。一個人可以專攻賭博、開鎖與偽造，另一個人擅長爆破、鬥毆與攀爬。

Medical skills are something that more than one character should possess. All the skills in the world don’t amount to anything if a character’s flat on his back, too weak to use them. The more characters who possess medical savvy, the safer the party is.

醫療類的技能應該不只一個人會。一個人倒在地上、虛弱到什麼都做不了的時候，天下的技能都幫不上忙。懂醫療的人越多，隊伍越安全。

Since you must have a minimum IQ to be able to learn certain skills, the higher you IQ, the more skills you get to choose from. You simply can’t learn the more technical skills if you’re not smart enough.

有些技能要智力到某個門檻才學得起來，所以智力越高，可挑的技能越多。不夠聰明就是學不了比較技術性的那些。

LVL: The level of your skill. The higher this is, the better you are. You can invest skill points in any skill as many times as you like, each time you invest in a skill, the skill level goes up Skills also improve as you use them. Since the cost of each successive skill level rises dramatically, improving a skill by using it is a cheap, effective method of gaining skill levels.

LVL：技能等級。越高越強。同一個技能想投入幾次技能點數都可以，每投一次等級就升一級。技能也會因為使用而進步；而每升一級的費用漲得很兇，所以「靠用它來練」是便宜又有效的升級辦法。

NOTE: When creating a character or when visiting a library, you’ll see the total available skill points at the top of the screen. (Your skill point value is the same as your initial I Q value). To select a skill, enter its number. If you make an error, you can start your selecting over again by pressing <CONTROL>R. Press <ESC> to exit this character profile entirely.

注意：建立角色或造訪圖書館時，畫面上方會顯示可用的技能點數總額（技能點數等於你最初的智力值）。要選一個技能就輸入它的編號。選錯了按 <CONTROL>R 重新開始選。按 <ESC> 直接離開這張角色名片。

IQ: The minimum IQ you must have to use this skill.

IQ：使用這個技能所需的最低智力。

PTS: How many skill points it will cost to acquire this skill. Initially, you can select a skill more than once; in fact, this is how you become more adept (or advance in levels) at this skill. Once you select a skill, the point cost doubles as each successive skill level becomes more costly.

PTS：取得這個技能要花多少技能點數。一開始同一個技能可以選很多次——事實上這正是把它練得更精（升級）的方法。選過一次之後，點數費用就加倍，往後每一級都更貴。

Don’t be stingy with your skill points when you’re creating your characters. You’ll have the chance to acquire more skill points as you move up in the ranks (see “Getting Promoted” for details on how you can accumulate skill points). You can save a few, if you’d like, since there are several special places outside of the Ranger Center where you can learn more skills. But keep in mind that picking skills at the Ranger Center is a one-shot deal; once you leave, you can’t come back to pick more.

建角色時別捨不得技能點數。階級往上升的時候還有機會拿到更多（怎麼累積見「升級」）。想留幾點也可以，因為遊俠中心之外還有幾個特別的地方學得到技能。但要記得：在遊俠中心挑技能只有一次機會，離開之後就回不來再挑了。

To reorder your skills, view them and press <CONTROL>R. Enter the number of he skill that you want at the top of the list, the skill that you want second, and so on until you’ve selected all the skills. The new list won’t appear until you’ve selected all the skills.

要重排技能順序，先叫出技能頁再按 <CONTROL>R。輸入你要排第一的那個技能的編號，接著是第二個，依此類推直到全部選完。全部選完之後新的清單才會出現。

除了輸入角色編號才叫得出來的資料之外，下面這幾項一直顯示在隊伍名單上。

## Getting Promoted（升級）

You’re awarded experience points each time you accomplish a difficult task or win a difficult battle. When you think you’re accumulated enough experience points to warrant a promotion, you should radio back to Ranger Center to have your instructors review your accomplishments. Since it doesn’t cost you anything to radio in, you can do it as often as you like. If you’ve progressed enough (and only your instructors know when this is), you’re given a field promotion.

每完成一件難事、打贏一場硬仗，都會拿到經驗值。覺得累積得夠了、值得升一級的時候，用無線電回報遊俠中心，讓教官檢視你的成績。呼叫無線電不花錢，想叫幾次都行。進展夠了的話（只有教官知道什麼時候夠），就會給你戰地晉升。

As a promotion reward, two points are automatically added to your maximum constitution (MAX, MAXCON). You’re also awarded an additional two points that you can add to any attribute your choose Put both points on the same attribute or put one point on two separate attributes. If you’d like, add them to maximum constitution to boost that value even higher.

晉升的獎勵是體力上限（MAX／MAXCON）自動加兩點，另外再給你兩點，可以加在任何一項屬性上：兩點都加在同一項，或分別加在兩項。想的話也可以加到體力上限，把它推得更高。

If you wish to use these two points to acquire a new skill, you must apply them to IQ. Remember, IQ points translate into skill points. If you don’t increase your skill points via IQ points, you won’t be able to add new skills.

想用這兩點換新技能的話，必須加在智力上。記住：智力點會轉成技能點。不透過智力去增加技能點，就加不了新技能。

## Recruiting Allies（招募夥伴）

Wasteland is populated with people who have, like you, survived the nuclear holocaust. From time to time you’ll run into non-player characters (NPCs) whom you can hire. You may wish to enlist their services to add skill, strength, or intelligence to your party. Refer to the Encounter command below on how to hire an NPC

荒野上住著跟你一樣熬過核浩劫的人。你會不時遇到可以雇用的非玩家角色（NPC）。你也許會想請他們入夥，替隊伍補上技能、力量或智力。怎麼雇用 NPC 見下面的 Encounter 指令。

NPCs are not robots who obey your every command. They don’t always do as told in combat. They don’t always trade something when you ask them to. While you may not like their autonomy, and think that they’re more trouble than they’re worth, remember that they may be able to get you things that you can’t get for yourself or get you into places where you can’t normally go.

NPC 不是有令必從的機器人。戰鬥中他們不見得照你說的做，你叫他們交出東西也不見得肯。你可能不喜歡他們這麼有主見，覺得麻煩大於價值；但別忘了，他們可能拿得到你自己拿不到的東西，也可能帶你進到平常進不去的地方。

To dismiss an NPC, use the disband command.

要遣散一個 NPC，用 disband（分隊）指令。
