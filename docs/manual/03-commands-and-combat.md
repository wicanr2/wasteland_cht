## Commands（指令）

During normal play, a menu of commands are listed across the button of the screen:

一般遊玩時，畫面底部橫列著一排指令：

| 指令（原文） | 說明（原文） | 說明（繁中） |
|---|---|---|
| Use | This lets a character use an Item, Skill, or Attribute to accomplish a task. Enter a character number and select an Item, Skill or Attribute. Then, enter a direction in which the character will use the Item, Skill, or Attribute (press the spacebar if you want to use something right where you’re standing). Certain skills offer additional prompts. For example, when you use a medical skill, you will be asked which character you want to heal.<br><br>After you use something you may get the message “That doesn’t seem to work.” This suggests that this particular item or method doesn’t work, but that another might. Also, if you use a skill or attribute and it doesn’t seem to work -- but you’re really sure it should -- keep trying. Sometimes persistence pays off. | 使用：讓一個角色動用物品、技能或屬性去完成一件事。輸入角色編號，選物品、技能或屬性，再輸入要往哪個方向使用（想對腳下這一格用就按空白鍵）。有些技能會再多問一句，例如用醫療技能時會問你要治誰。<br><br>用完之後可能出現「That doesn’t seem to work.」。這表示這件東西或這個方法不行，但換一個也許行。還有：某個技能或屬性用起來像是沒反應，而你確信它應該有用——那就再試幾次，有時候堅持是有回報的。 |
| Enc | Encounter calls up the same commands that are available to the party during combat. You may want to use Encounter to attract someone’s attention, so you can get information or try to hire them.<br><br>You can also use this command as a short cut to perform certain actions on several characters.. For instance, you could have a couple characters reload, while another uses an item. In this case, using Encounter would be faster than calling up each character individually. | 遭遇：叫出戰鬥時隊伍能用的那一組指令。你可能會用它來引起某人注意，好套情報或試著雇用對方。<br><br>它也可以當成對好幾個角色下令的捷徑。比方說讓兩個人裝填、另一個人用某件物品——這樣比逐一叫出每個角色快。 |
| Order | This changes the marching order of your party. There are times when you’ll want to change the order to maximize your party’s talents. For instance, if you’re travelling through a dangerous area, a character with high perception or silent movement would make an excellent point man. In another situation, a character carrying an item that could forestall an attack should be in the lead.<br><br>NOTE: When you select Order, you’re shown the current marching order. Select the number of the character you want in front, then second, then third, and so on until the whole party has been reordered. Being placed in the back of the ranks does not shield a character from combat. | 排隊：改變隊伍的行進順序。有時候換個順序能讓隊伍的長處發揮到最大。例如穿越危險地帶時，觀察或潛行高的人適合走在最前面；另一種情況下，身上帶著能先發制人的東西的人該打頭陣。<br><br>注意：選了 Order 之後會顯示目前的順序。依序選出你要排第一、第二、第三……的角色編號，直到全隊重排完畢。**排在後面並不會讓一個角色在戰鬥中比較安全。** |
| Disband | Disband lets you break your party into separate, smaller parties (up to four). This enables each party to travel independently of the other parties. If you have dead party members, use disband to bury them. You could also use disband when you’re under attack; one person could split off to distract the attackers. Some places can only be passed through by one character at a time, so each person will have to split off into their own party.<br><br>CAUTION: Before you can disband NPCs, you’re asked if you want them permanently dismissed. If you answer “yes,” then they are gone forever -- you’ll never , ever see them again. If you want them disbanded -- and not dismissed -- answer “no.”<br><br>When you select Disband, enter the numbers for those characters who wish to split off from the main party, and press <RETURN>. Next, indicate the direction that the disbanded party will move. Use View to shift control between parties.<br><br>To reunite separate parties into a single band, move them onto the same spot and they will automatically regroup. | 分隊：把隊伍拆成幾支較小的隊伍（最多四支），讓每一支各走各的，想拉多開都可以。分隊在戰鬥裡也有不少戰術上的好處。<br><br>分隊有幾個重要用途。**隊裡有人死了，用分隊把他葬了。** 遭到攻擊時也可以用：分一個人出去引開攻擊者。有些地方一次只容一個人通過，那就每個人各自成一隊。<br><br>警告：要分出 NPC 之前，遊戲會問你是不是要永遠遣散他。答「yes」他就永遠走了——你再也見不到他。只是要分隊而不是遣散的話，答「no」。<br><br>選了 Disband 之後，輸入要離開主隊的角色編號，按 <RETURN>，接著指出分出去那一隊要往哪個方向走。用 View 在各隊之間切換控制權。<br><br>要把分開的隊伍併回一支，把他們移到同一格上就會自動集合。 |
| View | View lets you shift control between parties. If you have more than two parties, and you’re not in control of the party you want, keep entering V until you reach the right one. If the party is on another map, you’re asked to confirm if you want to shift to them. | 換隊：在各支隊伍之間切換控制權。隊伍超過兩支而目前操作的不是你要的那一支，就一直按 V 直到切到為止。那一支在另一張地圖上時，會問你要不要切過去。 |
| Save | Saves the game.<br><br>Use this command anytime you find something you don’t want to lose. Even before the great nuclear war, computers were known to shut down at inopportune moments. See the Command Summary section for more important details on the Save command. | 存檔：把進度存下來。<br><br>拿到任何你不想弄丟的東西時就用它。早在那場核子大戰之前，電腦就以「總在不該關的時候關掉」聞名了。存檔的其他重要細節見「指令總覽」。 |
| Radio | Contacts your instructors at Ranger Center so they can evaluate your performance. If you’re done well, you’re given a field promotion. When you select Radio, you’re asked to confirm your choice. If you choose to continue, all your characters are evaluated at the same time and are apprised of any changes in rank. Because a Ranger once slept through his promotion, the instructors now require that all characters be conscious for this procedure; consequently, any injured, unconscious Rangers will be passed over. Promoted characters get a chance to modify attributes at this point (see “Getting Promoted” for more details). | 無線電：聯絡遊俠中心的教官，讓他們評估你的表現。表現夠好就給你戰地晉升。選了 Radio 會先要你確認；確認之後全隊同時受評，階級有變動的會被告知。由於曾經有個遊俠**睡過了自己的晉升**，教官現在要求受評時所有人都得清醒——所以受傷昏迷的遊俠會被跳過。升級的角色這時可以調整屬性（詳見「升級」）。 |

## Combat（戰鬥）

Often, when somebody or something comes into your range, you will enter the Encounter mode. An encounter is frequently the precursor to a fight. Your party roster and a text window will appear. The text window will contain a short description of the encounter; press <RETURN> when you’re done reading it. (If you want to temporarily remove the party roster and text window to see where the encounter is located, press the spacebar. To return to the party roster and text window, press any key.)

有人或有東西進入你的範圍時，通常就進入遭遇模式。遭遇往往是打起來的前奏。畫面會出現隊伍名單與一個文字視窗，視窗裡有這場遭遇的簡短描述；讀完按 <RETURN>。（想暫時把名單與文字視窗收掉、看看遭遇發生在哪裡，按空白鍵；按任意鍵回到名單與文字視窗。）

Your options during an encounter are:

遭遇中你有這些選項：

| 選項（原文） | 說明（原文） | 說明（繁中） |
|---|---|---|
| Run | This lets the entire party or a single character run towards or away from an encounter. Enter the direction that you want the party or character to move. If a single character successfully runs away, the he’s disbanded from the party (This is impossible if there are four separate parties already). | 逃跑：讓全隊或單一角色朝遭遇跑過去或跑開。輸入要移動的方向。單一角色成功逃走的話，他就從隊伍裡分了出去（已經有四支隊伍時做不到）。 |
| Use | This lets a character use an Item, Skill or Attribute to accomplish a task. Depending on which you choose a list will appear. You’re then asked to enter a direction in which the Item, Skill, or Attribute will be executed. | 使用：讓一個角色動用物品、技能或屬性去完成一件事。依你選的類別會出現一份清單，接著問你要往哪個方向施用。 |
| Hire | Not everybody you meet has turned hostile under the hot desert sun. Some are quite pleasant, and may even offer you valuable services. If you meet an NPC that you would like to try to hire, enter the number of the desired recruit. | 雇用：不是每個在烈日沙漠下遇到的人都變得有敵意。有些相當友善，甚至能幫上大忙。遇到想雇用的 NPC，輸入他的編號。 |
| Evade | This makes you much harder to hit in combat. Use this command when your comrades initiate a firefight, and you don’t have a missile weapon, or when you’re injured and wish to minimize the risk of further physical abuse. | 迴避：讓你在戰鬥中難打中得多。同伴開火而你手上沒有射擊武器時、或者你已經受傷不想再挨打時，用這一個。 |
| Attack | In the battle-ridden wastelands, this is a command you’ll often use. When you select it, you’re given a choice of targets. If your weapon is an automatic weapon capable of selective fire, your’re asked if you want to fire Single, Burst or Autofire. If the encounter is at an extended range, and you have no missile weapons, you’re told that there are no attackers within your range. If there’s only one group of attackers, they’re targeted automatically. | 攻擊：在戰亂不休的荒野，這是你最常用的一個。選了之後可以挑目標。武器是能選擇射擊模式的自動武器時，會問你要單發、點放還是全自動。遭遇距離較遠而你沒有射擊武器的話，會告訴你射程內沒有攻擊者。只有一群攻擊者時會自動鎖定他們。 |
| Weapon | Lets you ready a new weapon, which is useful when your gun jams in the heat of the battle and you don’t want to fix it. Or, once you assess the current battle situation, you may want to use a weapon that’s more effective. | 換武器：換一把武器備用。打到一半槍卡住而你不想當場修時很有用；或者看清戰況之後，想換一把更有效的。 |
| Load/unjam | Loads or unjams a weapon. This command takes a full combat round, so it’s a good idea to keep your weapon loaded at all times, otherwise you’ll have to reload in the first round of a firefight. (You can load or unjam at any time by selecting Enc from the Main Menu, or by choosing the weapon or appropriate ammo.) When a weapon jams, you lose the ammo that was in the weapon at the time. Unjamming a weapon during combat automatically reloads it. | 裝填／排除卡彈：裝填武器或排除卡彈。這個指令要花掉一整個戰鬥回合，所以最好隨時保持滿彈，否則交火第一回合就得裝填。（任何時候都可以從主選單選 Enc，或直接選那把武器或對應的彈藥來裝填或排除卡彈。）武器卡彈時，當時在裡面的彈藥會損失。戰鬥中排除卡彈會自動重新裝填。 |

Press <ESC> if you wish to return to a previous character’s options. Once you’ve made all your combat selections you’re asked if you want to execute the commands you chose. Press N to abandon your choices and start the procedure over again. Press Y if you’re satisfied. You’ll then see a rundown on the effects of the encounter in the text window. Use <CONTROL>K or the up arrow to speed up text scrolling. Use <CONTROL>J or the down arrow to slow down text scrolling.

想回到前一個角色的選項就按 <ESC>。全部下完令之後會問你要不要執行；按 N 放棄重來，按 Y 執行。接著文字視窗會逐條交代這場遭遇的結果。<CONTROL>K 或上方向鍵加快捲動，<CONTROL>J 或下方向鍵放慢。

Since parties can disband and more to different maps, there will be times when one party is locked in combat while another party is on an entirely different map. In these instances, the game asks if you want the other parties to enter into an encounter. If you answer “yes,” you’ll get one combat round for each disbanded party. This is a slow and time consuming process, so you should only run combats on multiple maps in emergencies. If you decline the invitation to have combat on multiple maps, the party you are currently viewing will execute their combat round before you can view the other parties.

隊伍可以分開、也可以走到不同的地圖上，所以有時候一支隊伍陷在戰鬥裡，另一支卻在完全不同的地圖上。這種時候遊戲會問你要不要讓其他隊伍也進入遭遇。答「yes」的話，每一支分出去的隊伍各跑一個戰鬥回合。這個過程又慢又費時，所以只有緊急狀況才該讓多張地圖同時開打。你婉拒的話，目前正在看的那一隊會先跑完自己的回合，你才切得到其他隊伍。

### Hand-to-Hand Combat（近身戰鬥）

Because the Rangers are trained soldiers, they have an advantage over the citizens in hand-to-hand combat. In general, hand-to-hand combat is not as deadly as a firefight, but you can still easily die from a good throttling or from a dead-on punch. Hand-to-hand combat can only take place at a distance of 14 feet or less.

遊俠是受過訓練的軍人，近身戰鬥比一般居民佔優勢。整體來說近身戰鬥不像交火那麼致命，但被好好掐一次、或吃到一記結實的拳頭，一樣很容易送命。近身戰鬥只發生在十四呎以內。

### Missile Weapon Combat（射擊戰鬥）

Missile weapon combat is combat that takes place with weapons capable of range fire. From guns to grenades, there are numerous missile weapons at your disposal. Area-effect weapons, like TNT, plastic explosives, and grenades, are auto-loading, which means that each time you use one, you automatically get another until your supply runs out, so you won’t be unarmed in the middle of a fight. Anti-tank weapons such as LAW rockets must be re-equipped after firing Automatic weapons -- assault rifles and submachine guns -- can shoot Single, Burst, or Autofire. All guns are clip fed and come in .45, 9mm, or 7.62mm calibers. Always carry the proper ammo unless you want to pistol-whip your enemies.

射擊戰鬥是用能遠距離射擊的武器打的仗。從槍到手榴彈，你手上的射擊武器種類不少。範圍型武器——TNT、塑膠炸藥、手榴彈——會自動補上：每用掉一個就自動遞上下一個，直到存量用完，所以你不會打到一半手上空空。反戰車武器如 LAW 火箭，射完之後要重新裝備。自動武器——突擊步槍與衝鋒槍——可以單發、點放或全自動。所有的槍都用彈匣供彈，口徑分點四五、九釐米與 7.62 釐米。隨身帶對的彈藥，除非你打算拿槍托敲敵人。

Keep in mind that all missile combat is simultaneous. Sure, it’s exciting when you burn a clip of AK-97 ammo into an onrushing horde of mutant bikers, but your excitement may diminish somewhat when you find that the mutants are returning fire with equal fervor. You shoot, they shoot.

記住：所有的射擊戰鬥都是同時發生的。把一整個彈匣的 AK-97 子彈潑向衝過來的一群變種機車幫當然很痛快，但發現那群變種人也以同等的熱情回敬時，痛快大概就會少一點。你開槍，他們也開槍。

### Selective Fire（射擊模式）

Single, Burst and Autofire have different effects. Single uses one bullet per round, which is great if you’re low on ammo, but not so great if you need to hit your target with the first shot. Burst consumes three bullets per round and gives the shooter a slight advantage in hitting and killing his target. Autofire uses up all the bullets in the clip, which greatly increases the chance of hitting a particular target and others in close proximity. A good choice if you really need to kill somebody, but not so good if you’re looking at your last clip.

單發、點放與全自動的效果各不相同。單發每回合一發子彈，彈藥吃緊時很好用，但你需要第一槍就命中時就沒那麼好。點放每回合三發，讓射手在命中與擊殺上略佔便宜。全自動把彈匣裡的子彈全部打光，大幅提高命中特定目標與周圍其他人的機會——真的非殺掉某人不可時是好選擇，但你手上只剩最後一個彈匣時就不是了。

### Explosives（爆裂物）

If you want to do a great deal of damage over a wide area, go with explosives. You get more impact per target area, which can be a lifesaver when being eyed hungrily by a pack of nuke pooches. Remember, ammo doesn’t rain from the heavens. Running your gun on full auto all the time may kill the bad guns in a hurry, but you’re likely to run out of ammo just as the last nuke pooch lunges for your throat.

想在大範圍造成大量傷害，就用爆裂物。單位面積的殺傷力較高，被一群核輻射野狗飢餓地盯著時可能救你一命。記住，彈藥不會從天上掉下來。整場都開全自動的確殺得快，但很可能就在最後一隻野狗撲向你喉嚨的那一刻，子彈剛好用完。
