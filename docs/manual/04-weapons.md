## Weapons List（武器表）

Missile weapons come in three range categories : short, medium, and long. You’ll find that using a clip pistol to hit a target 80 feet away is not too effective. Be sure to choose the appropriate weapon for the appropriate distance. Here are some of the more common weapons and their characteristics.

射擊武器分三種射程：短、中、長。你會發現拿手槍去打八十呎外的目標並不怎麼有效。距離對上武器才行。以下是幾把比較常見的武器與它們的特性。

| 射程分類 | 武器 | 說明（原文） | 說明（繁中） |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long Range<br>長程 | AK-97 Assault Rifle<br>AK-97 突擊步槍 | Created in the image of the legendary AK-47, the AK-97 is the latest in the assault rifle line. It was created for the Comintern Combine nations of Europe and Africa, but saw large distribution throughout the world before the war. It is chambered for 7.62mm ammo and fires from a 30-bullet clip. This highly-respected weapon is fairly commonplace. | 以傳奇的 AK-47 為藍本，AK-97 是突擊步槍這一系的最新型。它是為歐非的共產國際聯盟諸國造的，但戰前就已行銷全球。使用 7.62 釐米彈藥，三十發彈匣。這把頗受敬重的槍相當常見。 |
| Long Range<br>長程 | M1989A1 NATO Assault Rifle<br>M1989A1 北約突擊步槍 | The M1989A1 is big brother to the original M16A1. It was rechambered to accept the same 7.62mm ammo that the AK series of rifles uses so troops could use captured Soviet ammo. Although assault rifle users greatly preferred the American ammo for its damage capabilities, the military implemented this change in anticipation of front-line ammo shortages. With this arrangement, a solider could kill an enemy and replenish his supply at the same time. | M1989A1 是原本 M16A1 的老大哥。它改膛以吃 AK 系列同樣的 7.62 釐米彈藥，好讓部隊能用繳獲的蘇聯子彈。雖然使用者普遍偏好美製彈藥的殺傷力，軍方仍為了預防前線彈藥短缺而做了這個改動。這麼一來，士兵殺掉敵人的同時也補了自己的存量。 |
| Long Range<br>長程 | M19 Rifle<br>M19 步槍 | The M19 rifle is a single-shot weapon firing an 8-shot clip of 7.62mm ammo. When rumors of a Soviet invasion of the U.S. spread through the states, survivalists demanded a weapon that could use Soviet ammo (necessary, said the survivalists, if Americans were to be able to use captured ammo). The invasion never took place, but the M19 remains a high-profile weapon. Although it can’t fire more than one bullet at a time, it does have greater range than pistols. | M19 步槍是單發武器，用八發的 7.62 釐米彈匣。蘇聯即將入侵美國的傳聞在各州流傳時，生存主義者要求一把能用蘇聯彈藥的槍（他們說，美國人要用得了繳獲的子彈，這是必要的）。入侵始終沒有發生，M19 倒是一直很出名。它一次只打得出一發，射程卻遠勝手槍。 |
| Long Range<br>長程 | M17 Carbine<br>M17 卡賓槍 | The M17 Carbine is a lighter, short-barreled version of the M19 rifle. Its 10-shot clip of 7.62mm ammo gives it a faster fire rate than its big brother, but the barrel length makes it slightly less accurate at a maximum range. | M17 卡賓槍是 M19 步槍的輕量短管版。十發的 7.62 釐米彈匣讓它的射速比老大哥快，但槍管長度使它在最大射程上略遜一籌。 |
| Long Range<br>長程 | LAW Rocket<br>反戰車火箭 | The LAW rocket is a small, armor-piercing explosive rocket contained in a disposable firing tube. It was first developed to destroy tanks. The mass of the tip (made from depleted uranium) enables the rocket to blast through the armor so the explosive shell can burst, once inside. Some knowledge or skill in anti-tank weaponry is useful when employing rocket. | 反戰車火箭（LAW）是一枚裝在拋棄式發射管裡的小型穿甲爆裂火箭，最早是為了摧毀戰車而開發的。彈頭（貧鈾製）的質量讓火箭穿得透裝甲，爆炸彈體才能在裡面炸開。用火箭時，懂一點反戰車武器的知識或技能會有幫助。 |
| Medium Range<br>中程 | MAC 17 SMG<br>Mac 17 衝鋒槍 | The Mac 17 is the sturdiest of the compact submachine guns developed in the early 1990s. It shoots .45 Caliber slugs from a 30-shot clip and is known for its man-stopping capabilities. This small weapon is well-suited for close-up firefights. | Mac 17 是一九九〇年代初開發的緊緻型衝鋒槍裡最耐操的一把。三十發彈匣，發射點四五彈頭，以撂倒人的能力著稱。這把小槍很適合近距離交火。 |
| Medium Range<br>中程 | UZI 27 SMG<br>烏茲衝鋒槍 27 型 | The Uzi comes from the highly-skilled gunsmiths of Trans-Palestine. Its magazine holds 40 9mm bullets and is capable of burning a full clip in five seconds. This weapon was built specifically for fighting terrorists has has proven very effective on mutant bikers or religious fanatics. | 烏茲出自泛巴勒斯坦技藝高超的槍匠之手。彈匣裝四十發九釐米子彈，五秒內可以打光一整匣。這把槍本是為了對付恐怖分子而造，實戰證明拿來對付變種機車幫或宗教狂熱分子也很有效。 |
| Short Range<br>短程 | 1911A1 .45 Pistol<br>1911A1 點四五手槍 | The 1911A1 was the standard sidearm of the previous two world wars. The .45 slug, held in an 8-shot clip, was first developed to stop Moro rebels in the Phillipines. In its current role, it has no trouble slowing down desert raiders or the occasional mutant cactus. Many Desert Rangers find this a perfect weapon to take with them when they first head into the wastelands. | 1911A1 是前兩次世界大戰的制式配槍。八發彈匣裡的點四五彈頭，最初是為了阻止菲律賓的摩洛族叛軍而開發的。在如今的角色裡，它要拖慢沙漠劫掠者或偶爾出現的變種仙人掌毫無問題。許多沙漠遊俠第一次踏進荒野時，都覺得帶這把最合適。 |
| Short Range<br>短程 | VP91Z 9mm Pistol<br>VP91Z 九釐米手槍 | As well liked as the .45 pistol by Desert Rangers, the VP91Z is a variation on the West German design, featuring an 18-shot capacity of this weapon reduces the need to reload, making it ideal for extended firefights. | 在沙漠遊俠之間與點四五手槍一樣受歡迎。VP91Z 是西德設計的變型，十八發的容量減少了裝填的次數，很適合長時間的交火。 |
| Short Range<br>短程 | Grenades<br>手榴彈 | Grenades are small, hand-tossed explosive devices useful for inflicting damage on an entire group of foes at one time. Grenades have a wide area of effect, so be sure your character has a good throwing arm! | 手榴彈是小型的手擲爆裂裝置，能一次對一整群敵人造成傷害。作用範圍很廣，所以先確定你的角色手臂夠力！ |
