## INJURIES AND DEATH（傷勢與死亡）

Death is permanent. If your characters get seriously injured, get them medical attention quickly or kiss them goodbye.

死亡是永久的。角色受了重傷就趕快送醫，不然就跟他吻別吧。

If a character is lightly wounded, they’ve taken a few hits, but are still awake and functional. A few points have been knocked off his constitution.

輕傷的角色只是挨了幾下，還醒著、還能動，體力掉了幾點而已。

Characters become unconscious when their constitution dips below 1. While unconscious, they are unable to move. Over time, an unconscious character will regain consciousness and heal to full health. An alternative to waiting is to find a hospital and pay for a quick heal.

體力掉到 1 以下，角色就昏迷。昏迷時他不能動。時間久了，昏迷的角色會醒來並自行痊癒。不想等的話，找一家醫院付錢快速治療。

Seriously-wounded characters are in a whole different class of medical trouble. Like unconscious characters, whey can do nothing, but unlike unconscious characters, they don’t heal with time -- they worsen. Without medical help, seriously-wounded characters deteriorate to critical, to mortal, to comatose, and then to dead. Another character with medical skills may be able to stabilize the wounded character enough so he can improve to the unconsciousness category. If this happens, the character will recover with time.

重傷是完全不同等級的麻煩。與昏迷一樣什麼都做不了，但不同的是——他們不會隨時間好轉，只會惡化。沒有醫療介入的話，重傷會一路變成危急、瀕死、昏死，然後死亡。隊上有醫療技能的人也許能把傷者穩住，讓他退回昏迷那一級；退回去之後，時間久了就會復原。

Remember that time waits for no one. Disbanding one character and sending him off across the desert to find a doctor will not freeze time for a seriously-wounded character. When that character finally returns with a doctor, they may be back in time to bury the body. (This is why your party should contain somebody -- preferably two -- with medical skills.) Should the worst happen, you can bury a dead character by using the Disband command.

記住，時間不等人。分一個人出去、讓他穿越沙漠找醫生，並不會讓那個重傷者的時間停下來。等他帶著醫生回來，可能剛好趕上收屍。（這就是為什麼隊上該有人——最好兩個——會醫療技能。）真的碰上最壞的情況，用分隊（Disband）指令把死者葬了。

## TIME AND DISTANCE（時間與距離）

The maps in Wasteland vary in scale. The large desert map contains the various city maps, which, in turn, contain building maps or whole underground levels of sewers and hideouts. In combat, distances may seem a bit off for the map you’re on, but these are tactical distances and are valid in combat only.

《荒野遊俠》的地圖比例尺各不相同。大張的沙漠地圖裡包著各個城鎮地圖，城鎮地圖裡又包著建築物地圖、或整層的下水道與藏身處。戰鬥中的距離看起來可能與所在地圖對不太上，但那是戰術距離，只在戰鬥裡有效。

Because of the difference in scale of the maps, time passes differently on them. Though a single keystroke on the computer will move you one space north in both the desert and in a building, the amount of time that each move takes is different.

因為比例尺不同，各張地圖上時間流逝的速度也不同。在沙漠與在建築物裡，同樣按一次鍵都是往北走一格，但這一步花掉的時間並不一樣。

If you want time to pass without moving, press <ESC>.

想讓時間過去但不移動，按 <ESC>。

## SPECIAL PLACES（幾個特別的地方）

You begin your adventure in the desert along the California-Arizona border. The area you’ll cover extends from the Ranger Center in the south up to the Grand Canyon in the north. The towns of Quartz, Needles and Las Vegas are three places you’ll surely want to visit.

你的冒險從加州與亞利桑那州交界的沙漠開始。你會走過的範圍，南起遊俠中心、北至大峽谷。石英城、針岩城與拉斯維加斯這三個城鎮，你一定會想去看看。

### Little Old Quartz（小小的石英城）

Quartz is a small town that suffered extensive damage from the nuclear attacks, yet managed to survive. Although located far from the large military targets, this tiny community was affected by earthquakes and low-level nuclear fallout. But in its feisty, small town way, Quartz quickly resumed normal life. About the only trouble Quarts has these days is with bandits.

石英城是個在核攻擊中受損嚴重卻活了下來的小鎮。雖然離大型軍事目標很遠，這個小聚落仍受到地震與低強度輻射落塵影響。但它以小鎮特有的那股韌勁，很快就恢復了日常。如今石英城大概只剩土匪這一個麻煩。

### Xenophobic Needles（排外的針岩城）

Needles is bigger than Quartz. Aside from the flood that resulted when Hoover Dam was hit, life has not changed much in Needles. Needles is big enough to defend itself from roving bandits. However, it has become home to a couple of odd cults. The folks in Needles don’t really like strangers that much, but they’ve welcomed Desert Rangers in the past and are always willing to trade goods with them.

針岩城比石英城大。除了胡佛水壩被擊中造成的那場洪水之外，這裡的生活變化不大。針岩城大到足以自保、擋得住流竄的土匪，但也成了幾個古怪教派的地盤。針岩城的人不太喜歡陌生人，不過以前倒是歡迎過沙漠遊俠，也一向願意跟他們做買賣。

### Lovely Vegas（迷人的維加斯）

Vegas is the city of Las Vegas. No one is quite sure how the Soviet missiles managed to miss the city, but most folks figure it was because the “house” was betting against a missile landing -- and no one wins against the house. There was an international rumor about some Russian general’s markers being torn up after the attack, but that has yet to be confirmed.

維加斯就是拉斯維加斯。沒人說得準蘇聯的飛彈怎麼會偏過這座城，多數人認為是因為「莊家」押了飛彈不會落地——而沒有人贏得了莊家。國際上曾傳聞某位俄國將軍的欠條在攻擊之後被撕掉了，不過至今未獲證實。

Undaunted by its brush with perdition, Vegas is still open for business and does very well for itself. The city is large enough to avoid conquest, and many elements of the desert rabble have even found gainful employment as enforcers or casino employees. Despite the relative success of the city, however, there are dark rumors about horrors descending from the north to taking up residence in the city sewers. All may not be extremely well in the jewel of the Desert.

與毀滅擦身而過並沒有嚇到維加斯，它照樣營業，而且做得有聲有色。這座城大到不會被征服，沙漠上不少烏合之眾甚至在這裡找到了打手或賭場員工的正經差事。不過城市相對繁榮的同時，也流傳著陰暗的說法：有可怕的東西從北方下來，住進了城裡的下水道。這顆沙漠明珠的情況，也許不像表面那麼好。

### Deadly Deserts（致命的沙漠）

The desert is just as formidable as it was before the holocaust. In addition to the lack of water and the fierce heat, there are reports of radiation zones that have melted the flesh of unwary travellers. Along with the irradiated human element -- left-over biker gangs, rabid survivalists, crazed religious zealots -- all sorts of mutant animals make their home in the desert. Some say that travelling in the desert nowadays without an experienced guide is plain suicidal.

沙漠和核浩劫之前一樣難纏。除了缺水與酷熱，還有輻射區的傳聞——那些地方把不小心的旅人的血肉熔掉。除了受輻射影響的人類（殘存的機車幫、狂躁的生存主義者、發瘋的宗教狂熱分子），各式各樣的變種動物也以沙漠為家。有人說，如今沒有老練的嚮導就進沙漠，根本是自殺。
